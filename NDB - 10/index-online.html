Country Portfolio Evaluation
Republic of India
The Independent Evaluation Office (IEO) is delighted to present the India country portfolio evaluation (CPE)
In a CPE, IEO assesses the results of NDB’s body of work and partnership in a country and generates lessons and recommendations for strengthening the partnership in the future. The India CPE is the first CPE undertaken by IEO.
Since 2016, NDB has financed 26 projects in the India valued at USD 8.6 billion. Overall, the CPE found that India’s priorities for sustainable development have been well-supported by NDB’s investments, in particular in the transport, and water and sanitation sectors. The establishment of the Indian Regional Office (IRO) in Gift City, Gujarat, in 2022, has also helped bring the Bank closer to its clients and end beneficiaries.
However, the CPE also noted that NDB’s non-lending activities, such as the provision of technical assistance, knowledge sharing and partnerships with development partners in India need strengthening; and recommends that NDB develop a country strategy to better target and guide the Bank’s activities in the country going forward.
Below you will find the full and final CPE report; the Evaluation Lens (which summaries the main findings from the evaluation) in English, Chinese, Portuguese and Russian; and the accompanying infographic, which presents the CPE in a visual way.
We hope you find the documents a useful resource to discover more about NDB’s work in India to date.
