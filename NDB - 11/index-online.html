Report on NDB's Development Results
ThThe Independent Evaluation Office is delighted to present the first edition of its Report on NDB's Development Results (RDR).
From its establishment in April 2022 until 2024, IEO conducted 13 independent evaluations, and the RDR brings together the findings from these evaluations to provide a consolidated assessment of operational results at the aggregate level. The projects evaluated represent approximately 56% of NDB's total disbursed financing by July 2024, a significant amount of its total financial commitments since the Bank’s establishment in 2015.
The RDR evaluates the relevance, effectiveness, efficiency, impact and sustainability of NDB-financed operations, offering insights into the strengths of its activities on the ground and areas requiring attention. The report finds that NDB-financed projects are generally performing well in promoting sustainable economic and social development, and are broadly aligned with national priorities and the Sustainable Development Goals, particularly in renewable energy, transport infrastructure, and water supply.
However, project efficiency could be improved, in particular by mitigating administrative and implementation delays, and enhancing the sustainability of investments through robust exit strategies. More attention also needs to be devoted to project monitoring and supervision as well as portfolio management more broadly. While NDB has demonstrated success in mobilising financing for large-scale projects (e.g. from national development banks), there is room for expanding co-financing with other multilateral development banks and international financial institutions.
The report also underscores the importance of NDB's non-lending activities, and calls for deeper attention to knowledge-sharing, provision of technical assistance and capacity-building.
