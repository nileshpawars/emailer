Thematic Evaluation
NDB Financing and Activities in the Energy Sector in South Africa
In 2025, the Independent Evaluation Office (IEO) of the New Development Bank (NDB) conducted a thematic evaluation of the Bank’s financing and activities in South Africa’s energy sector. The evaluation assessed both lending and non-lending support provided by NDB between 2016 and 2024, with the objective of documenting the results achieved, identifying lessons learned, and making recommendations for the way forward.
During the period under review, NDB financed five sovereign and non-sovereign operations in the energy sector in South Africa with total approved financing of USD 972.8 million across eight provinces. NDB was also successful in mobilizing co-financing from state-owned entities and other multilateral development banks.
The evaluation found that NDB’s investments have delivered substantial and measurable results. They contributed to the addition of approximately 1,600 megawatts of renewable energy capacity, the construction of 374 kilometers of transmission lines, and reduction in greenhouse gas emissions. Beyond energy infrastructure, the NDB’s supported projects facilitated employment generation of around 10,000 jobs, contributed to expansion of local manufacturing capacity as well as increased participation in the energy value chain.
At the same time, the evaluation identified areas where development effectiveness could be strengthened. Social and economic dimensions of energy investments are not yet systematically integrated or tracked across projects, limiting the ability to fully assess broader development outcomes. In addition, the relatively limited emphasis on non-lending activities such as knowledge management, technical assistance, and capacity-building has constrained opportunities for deeper sectoral engagement, learning, and visibility. The evaluation also noted scope to strengthen project supervision and monitoring practices, particularly in relation to capturing and using results information.
Based on these findings, the evaluation puts forward recommendations aimed at strengthening NDB’s future engagements in South Africa’s energy sector. These include developing a South Africa-NDB country strategy, enhancing the Bank’s financing product mixt, developing a more structured approach to energy sector knowledge management, prioritising transmission capacity for future investments, making deeper use of the Project Preparation Fund for project design and implementation support, and finally, using project Design and Monitoring Frameworks more strategically to for better impact at completion.
