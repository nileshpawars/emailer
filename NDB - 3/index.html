Preliminary experience in establishing NDB on-the-ground presence
The role of regional offices
NDB's first regional office opened in 2018 - just three years after the Bank began operations. Since this first office - the Africa Regional Centre (ARC) - opened that year in Johannesburg, NDB has opened another three regional offices or centres (ROCs): the Americas Regional Office in São Paulo in 2019 with a sub-office in Brasilia, the Eurasian Regional Centre in Moscow in 2020, and the Indian Regional Office in 2022. A dedicated desk was established at its Shanghai headquarters to manage its Chinese operations.
NDB's aims in having, from the outset, a strong on-the-ground presence in its founder member countries, were to ensure strengthened dialogue, improve project performance and engagement with its member countries.
So, six years after the opening of the first ROC, what has the experience been like for NDB?
Does the evidence show that the Bank's on-the-ground presence has achieved its objectives as mentioned above?
The Independent Evaluation Office (IEO) of NDB undertook an evaluation to find out - assessing the amongst other things whether ROCs are helping foster a "One Bank" culture, how well they are supporting operations, and whether there is enough support for them from HQ, in particular regarding IT systems and platforms.
IEO's findings are laid out in the documents below. We hope you find them interesting!
