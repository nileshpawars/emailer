Putian Pinghai Bay Offshore Wind Power Project
In 2016 the NDB Board approved RMB 2 billion of funding (approximately 40% of the total cost) for a project to install new wind turbines in Putian Bay, located on the Taiwan Strait off China’s eastern coastline. The project aimed, amongst other things, to: add 250 MW of energy capacity; generate 873 million kWh of electricity in the first year; and avoid an average of 869,900 tonnes of CO2 emissions annually. The project was completed in December 2021.
The project performance evaluation (PPE) by the Independent Office of Evaluation (IEO) assesses the results of the project and generate lessons for the way forward. Overall, we found that the project has been successful and contributed greatly to the generation of renewable power in both Fujian Province and China; and the project was noted for its innovation, with technology patents developed and subsequently used in other offshore wind projects in China.
Nevertheless, some aspects of the project could have been improved and our recommendations included, for instance, the need to ensure that such projects include a more robust theory of change and comprehensive monitoring of project progress as well as provision of technical assistance and capacity building for project implementation agencies.
