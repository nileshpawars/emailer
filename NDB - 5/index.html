Renewable Energy Sector Development Project
Republic of South Africa
In 2019, the NDB Board approved a ZAR 1.15 billion loan to finance four renewable energy sub-projects in the Northern Cape Province of South Africa. The project aimed to increase renewable energy generation, reduce carbon dioxide emissions, and enhance the energy efficiency of the economy.
The Project Performance Evaluation (PPE) conducted by the Independent Evaluation Office (IEO) of NDB assessed the project’s results and found that, overall, the project was successful, meeting its stated objectives and fostering sustainable development. Additionally, the project strengthened institutional arrangements established by the government to support energy sector development and introduced advanced technology, enhancing stability within the energy sector.
However, we believe that certain aspects of the project could have been improved; and in our recommendations, we suggest that NDB's renewable energy sector investments in South Africa should focus more strategically on its role as a development bank while fostering collaboration with technical institutions in the country to develop and share lessons and enhance visibility in the country and region. Additionally, the Bank should improve its project design and monitoring framework to ensure important country-specific aspects, such as transformative equity in South Africa, are adequately considered.
