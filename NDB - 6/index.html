Corporate-level Evaluation
In 2024, the Independent Evaluation Office (IEO) conducted a corporate-level evaluation (CLE) to assess the overall robustness of NDB’s policy framework in supporting the Bank to meet its mandate.
Overall, IEO found that NDB had acted quickly to build up its policy portfolio, with 63% of the current 48 policies developed in the first year; and since its foundation in 2016 the Bank has managed to build up a universe of policies and policy-related documents that provide useful guidance to Management and staff.
Nevertheless, as the Bank evolves more needs to be done to consolidate the Bank's framework.
This includes, for instance, ensuring consistency across policy documents in terms of standardised
formats and templates; filling key policy gaps in NDB priority areas; strengthening the quality assurance process; and establishing user-friendly IT platform for all guidance documents.
