MARK YOUR CALENDARS!
Year-end knowledge-sharing session based
on completed evaluations in 2024
by the Independent Evaluation Office
As in 2023, IEO is pleased to invite all NDB colleagues to its year-end knowledge-sharing session on 11 December 2024. The main objective of the session is to share the results and cross-cutting findings, lessons and recommendations from independent evaluations completed in 2024, as well as to stimulate a dialogue among NDB colleagues on the way forward.
Hybrid format:  in-person at Shanghai HQ / online for regional office colleagues
Event highlights
• Presentation of results/findings from all completed IEO evaluations in 2024
• Remarks by the Director General of IEO, and a representative of NDB Senior Management
• Interactive Q&A session
A packed lunch will be provided for participants at headquarters.
