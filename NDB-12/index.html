A Joint Learning Seminar by the Brazilian Monitoring and Evaluation Network and the BRICS Policy Centre PRIME Hub, supported by Independent Evaluation Office of the New Development Bank.

22nd October 2025
17:00 - 19:00 (BRT)
23rd October 2025
09:00 - 17:30 (BRT)
Rio Data Centre, Pontifical Catholic University of Rio de Janeiro (PUC-Rio), Brazil

At a time when infrastructure spending will shape climate and development outcomes for decades, how we evaluate these investments has never mattered more. This inaugural high-level seminar on the evaluation of infrastructure interventions will convene senior government officials, multilateral leaders, development finance institutions, academics, and civil society from BRICS and partner countries to address this gap.

Discussions will explore methodologies that integrate economic, social, environmental, and territorial perspectives, ensuring that infrastructure investments are inclusive, participatory, and climate-resilient. The agenda will feature case studies from the Global South, strategies to strengthen governance and accountability, and the launch of new tools for participatory evaluation.

With COP30 approaching, the seminar offers a timely platform to align evaluation frameworks with global climate and development commitments.

A formal invitation and detailed programme will follow.
