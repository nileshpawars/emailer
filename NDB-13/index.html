Stakeholders' Knowledge-Sharing Seminar
Thematic Evaluation of NDB Financing and Activities in the Energy Sector in South Africa
Organised by the Independent Evaluation Office (IEO) of the New Development Bank (NDB) in coordination with the NDB Africa Regional centre.

The main objectives of the seminar are to:

(i)
Discuss the findings, lessons and recommendations from the energy sector thematic evaluation report
(ii)
Reflect on key issues and explore opportunities for enhancing NDB’s and its partners’ capacities and engagement for future operations in energy sector in South Africa
(iii)
Promote cross-fertilisation of experiences, good practices, lessons and dialogue on topics of mutual interest with a variety of stakeholders

Participants will include: High-level government representatives from South Africa and other countries, key stakeholders of NDB financing and activities in the energy sector in South Africa, representatives from commercial banks with energy sector financing and activities in South Africa, representatives from private sector organisations, academic and research institutions, international development organisations with a presence in South Africa, and others.

When: Week of November 17 2025 (exact date TBC)

Where: Johannesburg or Pretoria (exact location TBC)
