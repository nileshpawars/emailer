【预留日期】
成果与经验分享研讨会
首次中国国别项目组合评价（CPE）
新开发银行（NDB）独立评价局（IEO）诚邀您预留日程，出席关于首次中国国别项目组合评价的成果与经验分享研讨会。会议将围绕本次评价的核心发现、经验教训与良好实践开展交流。
本次研讨会将搭建多方利益相关方对话与南南合作平台，基于 2015–2024 年新开发银行对华合作的经验与启示，探索深化可持续发展领域伙伴关系的路径。预计参会规模约 200 人，包括政府官员、新开发银行董事及管理层代表、省级主管部门、项目执行单位和利益相关方代表、发展伙伴机构，以及新开发银行成员国与其他合作机构代表。
日期：2026 年 9 月 23 日
时间：北京时间（UTC+8）09:00–17:00
地点：北京・新疆大厦（海淀区三里河路7号）
正式邀请函及详细会议安排将于适时发送，研讨会概念说明、议程及各环节材料后续将在独立评价局官网发布。

SAVE THE DATE
Results & Knowledge Sharing Seminar
First Country Portfolio Evaluation (CPE) for China
The Independent Evaluation Office (IEO) of the New Development Bank (NDB) is pleased to invite you to save the date for a results and knowledge sharing seminar on the findings, lessons learned and good practices from NDB’s first Country Portfolio Evaluation (CPE) for China.
The event will foster multi stakeholder dialogue and South South cooperation, drawing on insights from NDB’s partnership with China over 2015–2024 to explore pathways for stronger collaboration on sustainable development. Approximately 200 participants are expected, including government officials, NDB Board and Management representatives, provincial authorities, project stakeholders, development partners, and delegates from NDB member countries and partner institutions.
23 September 2026
09:00–17:00, Beijing Time (UTC+8)
Beijing, China | Xinjiang Plaza (No. 7 Sanlihe Road, Haidian District)
A formal invitation and detailed programme will be circulated in due course. The seminar concept note, agenda and session materials will subsequently be published on the IEO webpage.
