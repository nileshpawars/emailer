The Independent Evaluation Office (IEO) of the New Development Bank (NDB) has completed four major evaluations in 2023, marking a significant milestone in its commitment to ensuring transparency and accountability.
The evaluations completed signify a journey of strengthening accountability, learning, growth, and collaboration. Two further evaluations will be completed in the very near future, respectively, on NDB’s financial architecture and the Bank’s experience in setting up regional offices and strengthening on-the-ground presence.
