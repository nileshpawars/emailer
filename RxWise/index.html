Schizophrenia in Primary Care:
Enabling shared care team
A CFPC accredited program designed to support primary care providers caring for individuals with schizophrenia.
Earn up to 1.5 credits.
Program Objectives:
• Describe the evidence-based importance of assessing functional recovery in individuals living with schizophrenia to achieve optimal outcomes

• Explain how to use the PSP scale in tracking the functional recovery of individuals living with schizophrenia.

• Discuss how to incorporate the PSP into ongoing clinical follow-up in individuals living with schizophrenia.
